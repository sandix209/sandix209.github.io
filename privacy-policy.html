Pixel Parade – Privacy Policy

Developer: CometCraft (Babina Firma LTD, 71-75 Shelton Street, London WC2H 9JQ, UK) · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Pixel Parade is a puzzle game for Android and iPhone. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, heroes, settings) is saved on your phone. It does not leave your device unless you use the online features below.

Online features (leaderboard, Versus, friends)

To show leaderboards and play Versus matches, the game uses Google Firebase. It creates an anonymous account for your device (no name, email or phone number is needed) and stores:

  • a random player ID and your friend code,
  • the nickname and avatar you choose,
  • your scores, trophies and country (from your device settings) for the leaderboards,
  • your friends list and the state of a Versus match while you play.

Other players can see your nickname, avatar, country, scores and trophies. Firebase is operated by Google; see Firebase privacy and security.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between levels and when you choose to watch a video for a reward. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first; on iPhone it also asks whether ads may use tracking. You can change your choice at any time under Profile → Privacy choices (where it applies), in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), or by buying "No ads". See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation of what you bought so the game can deliver it.

Children

Pixel Parade is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Online data is kept while you play and deleted on request. To delete it, email us your friend code (Profile screen) and we will remove your player data within 30 days. Uninstalling the game deletes the progress stored on your device.

Security

Data sent online is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access to the online database is limited by security rules so players can only change their own data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
